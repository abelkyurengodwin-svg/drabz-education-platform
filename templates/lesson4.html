📊 Lesson 4: Support & Resistance

What Is Support?

Support is a price level where selling pressure may become weaker, causing price to slow down or move upward. Think of support as a floor that can help stop price from falling.

What Is Resistance?

Resistance is a price level where buying pressure may become weaker, causing price to slow down or move downward. Think of resistance as a ceiling that can stop price from rising.

Support and Resistance Together

Support and resistance help traders identify important areas where price may react. Price can move between support and resistance until it eventually breaks through one of them.

What Is a Breakout?

A breakout happens when price moves beyond an important support or resistance level. A strong breakout can show that the market may be starting a new move.

What Is a Retest?

After breaking a level, price may return to that same area before continuing its move. This return is called a retest.

💡 Important Note for Beginners

Support and resistance are areas, not always exact lines. Price can move slightly above or below a level before changing direction. Always combine them with other forms of analysis instead of relying on them alone.
