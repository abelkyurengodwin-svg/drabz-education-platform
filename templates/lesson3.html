🕯️ Lesson 3: Candlestick Basics

What Is a Candlestick?

A candlestick is a way of showing how price moved during a specific period of time. It shows where price opened, where it closed, and the highest and lowest prices reached.

🟢 Bullish Candle

A bullish candle means price closed higher than where it opened. It shows that buyers were stronger during that period.

🔴 Bearish Candle

A bearish candle means price closed lower than where it opened. It shows that sellers were stronger during that period.

📌 Candle Body and Wicks

The candle body shows the difference between the opening and closing prices. The wicks show the highest and lowest prices reached during that period.

💡 Why Candlesticks Matter

Candlesticks help traders understand price movement and recognize what buyers and sellers are doing in the market.
