Abel Kyuren Godwin

I'm Abel Kyuren Godwin, 18 years old, from Nasarawa State, and the founder of Abzilla FX Academy.

How Did I Discover Trading? 🤔

I was living with my parents when I was in JSS3, while my brother was living in Abuja and managing a laundry shop. Later, my mother asked me to go to Abuja to live with my brother and help him manage the laundry.

Everything was going well until my brother discovered trading around July 2024. He came across one of Jeffrey Benson's videos on YouTube, which motivated him to start learning about Forex trading.

He started with a demo account, and about a month later, he funded a real trading account with $50. At that time, he was still learning and had not yet developed a proper strategy or discipline.

Around June/July, he introduced me to trading, but at first I wasn't really serious about it. A few days later, he played one of Jeffrey Benson's videos, and I decided to watch.

After watching the video, I was amazed. I thought, "Wow, is this how Forex trading works?" 😃

That moment motivated me to start learning about trading. I began working on myself and went to YouTube to study trading, price action, strategies, and the fundamentals of the Forex market.

That was the beginning of my journey into trading.

My name is Auwal Usman. I'm 18yrs old, I am the founder of "Dr Dreamz FX Group" and from Kano State.

How Did I Discover Trading? 🤔

Well, my journey into trading started through my childhood friend Jammy Forex, also known as Jammy FX Academy. Through him, I was introduced to Forex trading and started developing an interest in learning more about the financial markets.

As I continued learning, I also came across Jeffrey Benson, whose teachings and content became another important influence in my trading journey.

My journey with Abel Kyuren Godwin began through WhatsApp because of our shared interest in trading. As we talked and shared ideas, we realized that we both wanted to help beginners understand trading before risking their money.

That idea eventually became DRABZ — a platform created to help beginners learn the basics of trading, understand the risks, and build knowledge before putting real money into the market.
