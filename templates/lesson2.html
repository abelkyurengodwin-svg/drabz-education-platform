Lesson 2 - Market Structure

Understanding how price moves in the market

What is Market Structure?

Market structure is the way price moves and forms patterns on a chart. It helps a trader understand whether the market is generally moving upward, downward, or sideways.

Before taking a trade, a trader can study the market structure to understand what price has been doing and where important changes may be developing.

The basic structures we will study are the uptrend, downtrend, and ranging market.

1. Uptrend 📈

An uptrend happens when price is generally moving upward and creating a series of Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL).

Higher High (HH)

A Higher High happens when price moves above the previous high and creates a new high.

Higher Low (HL)

A Higher Low happens when price pulls back but the new low remains above the previous low.

HL → HH → HL → HH

This type of structure shows that price is generally making higher levels as it moves upward.

2. Downtrend 📉

A downtrend happens when price is generally moving downward and creating a series of Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL).

Lower High (LH)

A Lower High happens when price makes a high that is below the previous high.

Lower Low (LL)

A Lower Low happens when price falls below the previous low and creates a new low.

LH → LL → LH → LL

This type of structure shows that price is generally making lower levels as it moves downward.

3. Ranging Market ↔️

A ranging market happens when price moves sideways instead of forming a clear series of higher highs and higher lows or lower highs and lower lows.

Price often moves between an upper area of resistance and a lower area of support.

When the market is ranging, it is important to recognize that there may not be a clear upward or downward trend.

4. Break of Structure (BOS)

A Break of Structure, commonly called BOS, happens when price breaks an important previous high or low in the market structure.

Traders study breaks of structure because they can provide information about how price is changing its current structure.

However, a break of structure should not automatically be treated as a signal to enter a trade. It should be studied together with the rest of the market analysis.

5. Why Market Structure Matters

Understanding market structure can help a trader:

  • Identify the current direction of the market.
  • Understand how price is currently moving.
  • Recognize higher highs, higher lows, lower highs, and lower lows.
  • Identify possible changes in market structure.
  • Combine market structure with other forms of analysis.
  • Avoid entering trades without first studying price movement.
Important: Market structure does not guarantee that a trade will be profitable. It is only one part of technical analysis. Proper risk management and disciplined decision-making are also important.

Beginner Reminder 🧠

Do not rush to trade simply because you see an uptrend, downtrend, or break of structure.

First learn to identify the structure correctly. Then combine it with your other analysis and always understand the risk involved before considering a trade.
