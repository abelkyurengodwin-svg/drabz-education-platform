📖 Lesson 1: Trading Fundamentals

Welcome to your first DRABZ trading lesson.

What is Trading?

Trading involves buying and selling financial instruments with the aim of benefiting from changes in their prices.

What is the Forex Market?

Forex, short for foreign exchange, is the global market where currencies are exchanged. Currency pairs such as EUR/USD represent the relationship between two currencies.

Important Trading Terms

  • Buy: Taking a position expecting price to rise.
  • Sell: Taking a position expecting price to fall.
  • Entry: The price where a trade is opened.
  • Stop Loss: A tool used to limit potential loss.
  • Take Profit: A level where a trader plans to close a profitable trade.

Risk Management

Learning to manage risk is an important part of trading. Never treat trading as guaranteed income, and understand the possibility of losing money before using real funds.

Steps to Follow If You Want to Become a Profitable Trader

1. Never Start Your Trading Day Blind

Never enter the market without preparation. Always spend at least 10–20 minutes analyzing the market before considering a trade.

2. Build Emotional Resilience

Trading requires both a good strategy and emotional discipline. Learn to control emotions such as fear, greed, and anxiety.

3. Don't Cut Your Winners Short

Many beginners close profitable trades too early because of fear, while holding losing trades for too long, hoping the market will reverse. Learn to follow your trading plan instead of acting on fear.

4. Know Your Emotional Weakness

Every trader has emotional weaknesses. Identify whether you struggle with greed, fear, anxiety, impatience, or overconfidence, and work on controlling it.

5. Spend Time Analyzing Your Trades

Try to spend as much time reviewing your trades as you spend trading.

Example: If you spend 30 minutes trading, spend around 30 minutes analyzing and reviewing those trades.

6. Follow Your Trading Rules

If you ignore your trading rules and still make a profit, don't consider that a successful trade. You broke your process.

7. Don't Overtrade as a Beginner

Avoid spending too many hours continuously trading. Long trading sessions can lead to mental fatigue and poorer decision-making.

8. Your Trading System Must Have an Edge

If your trading system is consistently unprofitable, you cannot expect consistent profitability from it. Test, review, and improve your strategy before risking significant money.

9. Some Days Have No Good Setups

Some trading days may pass without a setup that meets your strategy's rules. That's okay. Don't force a trade.

10. Avoiding Bad Trades Is Important

For beginners, not taking a bad trade can be just as important—and sometimes more difficult—than taking a winning trade.

11. Be Careful on Fridays

Market conditions can vary on Fridays. If you choose to trade, pay close attention to market conditions and your strategy's rules.

12. Pay Attention to Economic News

Major economic news can cause sudden and unpredictable market movements. Always keep track of important economic events, including FOMC announcements.

13. Avoid Choppy Markets

Be careful when the market is moving sideways without a clear direction. Choppy conditions can create false signals and traps.

14. Never Trade When You're Angry or Distracted

This is very important. Never take a trade when you are angry, stressed, tired, or distracted. Your emotions can affect your decision-making.

15. Stick to One Strategy

As a beginner, focus on learning and mastering one trading strategy instead of constantly jumping between different strategies.

16. Trade With the Trend

Trading with the overall trend can be easier to manage than constantly trying to trade against it.

Remember: The trend is your friend.

17. Always Use a Stop Loss

Always put a stop loss on your trades. A stop loss can help limit your potential loss if the market moves against your position.

Final Reminder

Trading is not about winning every trade. It is about having a plan, managing risk, controlling your emotions, and consistently following your strategy.

Be patient. Be disciplined. Protect your capital.
